Menu designer

The screen where a product owner redesigns the bot for a promo without engineering. Its whole difficulty is one requirement: the same menu renders two completely different ways — tappable buttons in direct mode, a numbered text list in business mode — so the designer shows both at once, side by side, and neither is treated as the “real” one. A menu that reads well as buttons and badly as text is a menu half your players will meet badly.

Everything reused is B1-DS verbatim. The shell is B6-S01's. Templates, variables and the command set are the rest of Section E and arrive next; this delivery is the menu itself.

Amharic is real text, flagged for native review. Menu labels are NA BET PLC's.

rows E01–E04, E12 · Doc 05 FR-27, §8, §9, §13

Delivery note

Delivered: E01 (§A both themes; the 12-button and 3-per-row limits as states in §B, both themes), E02 (target types on the selected button, §A both themes), E03 (dual preview, §A both themes, each frame labelled for screen readers), E04 (language tabs with completeness badges, §A both themes, plus the untranslated-label treatment in the dark frame), E05 (publish confirmation naming affected bots, and version history with restore — §B, both themes), E06 (empty menu and preview loading — §B, both themes), E12 (move-up / move-down beside every row, §A both themes), E13 (§C, both themes).

Not here: bot message templates, template variables, the command set, /language gating and the window-guidance copy are rows E07–E11 and arrive as the next screen. Instant rollback shares the version-history control shown here; the confirmation for it is the same publish dialog, since a rollback is a publish.

Deviations to record: the 375 variant is read-only behind the explicit notice, per your ruling. Recorded in row E13 of the contract and here. Nothing else departs from the documents.

One thing I want your eye on: in the Amharic tab I render untranslated buttons in English inside a dashed amber outline rather than hiding them or showing an empty slot — so the designer shows the mixed-language menu a player would actually receive. It is uglier than a clean list on purpose. If you would rather see the gap than the fallback, say so and I will invert it.
