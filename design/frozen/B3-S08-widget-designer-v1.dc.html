What the phone view is for

Checking, not configuring. An admin on a phone wants to know which widget is live, whether anything is unpublished, and whether a rollout is healthy — so the list, the version and the status badge come first and the editors stay behind them. Designing a full colour picker for a 375 screen would be effort spent on a session that almost never happens, and the live preview needs both device frames side by side to be worth anything.
