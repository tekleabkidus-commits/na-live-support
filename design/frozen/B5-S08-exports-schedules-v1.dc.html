Exports and schedules

An export is the moment a number leaves the platform's control. Once a PDF is in an email thread nobody can add a filter chip to it, so everything the screen would have told you has to be baked into the file itself — that is what Doc 15 FR-16's watermark and filter stamp are really for.

Schedules add a harder problem: they outlive the intent that created them. Doc 15 FR-17 freezes the owner's scope at save, so a scheduled report keeps meaning the same thing next quarter — and pauses rather than drifts when the owner leaves or a metric is retired.

§B

Schedules

I07 · I08 · I09 · I10 · I11 · I12

A schedule is a standing instruction to send data to people. Two things follow: it must say whose eyes the numbers are computed for, and it must stop rather than guess when that answer changes. Both failure modes here — a deactivated owner, a retired metric — pause the schedule and explain, instead of quietly sending something different.
