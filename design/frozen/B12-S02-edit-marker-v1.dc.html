Edit marker, revision history, tombstone

Doc 04 FR-21, FR-22, FR-23, §8, §13 · your rulings. The tombstone is the approved B1-DS component used verbatim, not redrawn. Four things: the edited marker on any bubble that has revisions; the revision history behind conversations.history.full, marker-only for everyone else; the neutral tombstone when a customer deletes; and edit/delete on staff's own messages only where the channel adapter supports it. AI messages never carry an edit affordance — corrections are new messages.
