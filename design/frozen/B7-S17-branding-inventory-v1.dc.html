Branding — Surface inventory

Doc 16 FR-02 says the preview is the checklist, and that is the whole design: eight themed surfaces rendered live from the draft, so “where does this logo appear?” is answered by looking rather than by remembering. Nothing on this page is a link to documentation. Every tile is the surface itself, small.

Five of the eight belong to other documents. They appear here because an operator rebranding at midnight needs to see everything their theme touches — but each one opens its owner's editor rather than a copy of it, and the tile says which document owns it. A branding page that quietly re-implemented the widget's appearance controls would be the worst kind of convenient.

Built on B1-DS v4. Tiles render the labelled fixture mark per the 3 August ruling. Parity checked against the file before this was written.

§B
