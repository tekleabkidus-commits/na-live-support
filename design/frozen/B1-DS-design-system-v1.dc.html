Design system — Step 1

Checklist rows A (foundations) and B (components). Read §1, tell me which colour direction you want, and the whole library re-skins to it. Everything in §2 is fixed by the documents and is identical in all three directions — you are only choosing the neutral base and the accent.

Amharic here is real text and is flagged for native review before build.

§1

Colour directions — pick one

checklist 001 · Doc 16 FR-01

Direction A — Slate

Cool blue-tinted greys · clear blue accent

Familiar helpdesk temperature. Cool neutrals keep long shifts calm, and the blue accent reads as “action”. Risk: the accent blue sits close to the fixed new status blue.

Direction C — Ink

Warm stone greys · near-monochrome ink accent My recommendation

Six hues are already spoken for by the fixed status set (Doc 06 §9). An ink accent collides with none of them, so colour in the workspace only ever means state. It is also the safest white-label base: when a tenant drops in their brand colour it takes the accent slot cleanly, without fighting a colour you already chose.
