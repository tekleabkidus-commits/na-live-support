§H — Contract (build notes on the frame)
J01–J06 · Q2/Q3 honored · X2 audited · §13 §14
Contract
· Q3 honored: every output is an artefact in a workflow — feed item, email, review card, queue row. No Doc 15 report pages exist on this screen.
· Q2/J06 honored: the RG queue renders only for the stricter RG permission; for anyone else this page carries no count, no nav row, no vocabulary that reveals it.
· X2 audited: every RG row shows the template-sent fact and its triggering excerpt; nothing here edits or generates RG text.
· Anomalies always state their baseline in the sentence; sentiment items always link into their thread and name who holds it.
· The pre-score is labeled advisory at every appearance and never renders outside a reviewer's queue; the human review is the score of record.
· Clears require a reason, feed Doc 08 EC-06 calibration, lift suppression, and audit to the RG log; confirms audit equally. Digest metrics keep digits and version ids verbatim through localization (§14).
