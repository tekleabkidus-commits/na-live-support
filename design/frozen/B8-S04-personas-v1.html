B8-S04-personas-v1 · Section D · checklist rows D01–D05 (+ X3 audited) · Doc 09 FR-18, FR-04, FR-19a, §8, §14 · permission ai.* per Doc 03

An operator shapes the assistant's voice here — its name, its greeting, how warm and how wordy it is, per language. The design's one conviction: you meet the change before your customers do. Every edit plays against the same five canned conversations in the live preview, and Publish stays locked until the pending change has been previewed. Publishing is instant and versioned; rollback is one click and equally instant (FR-04, the menu-publish pattern).

The disclosure is drawn as what it is: a sentence the operator may reword, attached to a lock they cannot open. The Backoffice shell is B6-S11 law — context only. Amharic is real, flagged for native review.

§G — Contract (build notes on the frame)
X3 X5-adjacent audited · §13 §14 · FR-04 FR-18
Contract
· X3 audited: the disclosure field edits wording only; the lock and its sentence are permanent UI — no state of this screen renders a disable control.
· Preview-before-publish is enforced in the UI (the FR-18 contract): Publish stays disabled, with the reason on the button, until all five canned conversations have been previewed for the pending draft.
· Tone sliders carry text values and labeled endpoints; keyboard-adjustable with the value announced (§13).
· Every publish/rollback is instant, versioned, attributed; decisions log the exact config version (FR-04, FR-20).
· The §14 gate blocks enabling, not drafting, and always names what is missing.
· Preview bubbles carry the ✦ label + "preview" so no capture of this screen can pass as a real conversation.
