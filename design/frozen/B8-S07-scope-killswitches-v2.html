Scope & kill switches

B8-S07-scope-killswitches-v2 · Section G · checklist rows G01–G06 · Doc 09 FR-18, FR-10, US-07, AC-08, SEC-05 · §8 §9 §13 · v2 — scope-limited reopen (closing-audit ruling, 27 Aug): ADDS the whole-grid all-on frame in §B; nothing else changed

Two rooms on one floor: the quiet one decides where the AI is allowed to work (which KB categories it may answer from, which endpoints run the chatbot), and the loud one turns it off right now. The kill-switch grid is designed for the worst minute — oversized targets you can hit while stressed, a confirmation that states the blast radius in that endpoint's own daily numbers, and an OFF state that stays loud everywhere: red in the grid, "OFF since {time} by {actor}" under the switch, and a banner mirrored into the inbox header until it's back on.

Each switch silences exactly its own cell — surface × channel × endpoint — and nothing beside it (AC-08's both-directions law, drawn as a grid where every cell is its own switch). Backoffice shell is B6-S11 law — context only.

§C — The confirmation: blast radius in the endpoint's own numbers · 1440 · light

Turn the chatbot OFF for @BrandSupport?

Blast radius: the chatbot stops answering on @BrandSupport — about 312 conversations/day. Every inbound goes straight to your team with the handoff template. Nothing else turns off: @BrandVIP, Copilot and Supervisor keep running.

Takes effect within 10 seconds. The switch stays red with your name and the time until someone turns it back on; the inbox header shows a banner meanwhile. High-visibility audit event.

The red verb repeats the exact scope being switched — a stressed operator confirms what they aimed at, not a generic "Confirm". The radius names what stays ON as prominently as what goes off.

Turn the chatbot back ON for @BrandSupport?

Resumes within 10 seconds. New inbounds get the chatbot again; conversations already with your team stay with your team. The inbox banner clears. Audited like the off.

Recovery is calmer — but still explicit that in-flight human conversations are not yanked back to the bot.

§H — Contract (build notes on the frame)
G01–G06 · AC-08 · SEC-05 · §13
Contract
· Every switch is role="switch" with its state in words ("On" / "OFF since…") — never color alone; keyboard reaches and flips every cell through the same confirm (§13).
· One switch, one cell, one radius: the confirm names what turns off AND what stays on; the grid shows both directions after the flip (AC-08).
· Off is loud in three places at once — the cell, the page banner, the inbox mirror — and every one carries {time} + {actor}.
· Flips take effect ≤ 10 s, are evented (ai.killswitch.changed) and audit as the high-visibility class (SEC-05); the strip on the page shows the recent history.
· Nothing pulses or blinks in any state; reduced motion changes nothing because there is no motion to reduce.
· Scope's internal-articles lock is structural (FR-10): the chatbot cannot retrieve internal content, so the UI offers no checkbox to try.
