FR-15 to FR-18, answered and folded in

You sent the four requirements my copy had truncated, and nothing in them was guesswork-compatible — three of the four changed rows I had written from cross-references.

  • FR-15 split one guessed row into four: in-hours, out-of-hours with the bot on, out-of-hours with it off, and the SMS that follows a phone number. The Doc 14 SMS row is new — I had no way to know it existed.
  • FR-16 was uploads, as I suspected, but it named drag, paste and picker as three paths and makes pasting a screenshot first-class. That is now its own row: it is how a deposit receipt actually arrives.
  • FR-17 was tighter than “after engagement” — the permission comes after the visitor's first sent message, and all three channels go silent when the thread is visible and focused. Two new rows.
  • FR-18 added the detail that matters to a designer: panel open-or-closed is per tab by design, while unread and sound are shared.

What I have deliberately left out

  • Doc 13 §3 puts the message pipeline (Doc 04), chatbot logic (Doc 09), KB content (Doc 10), form definitions (Doc 12) and inbox rendering (Doc 07) out of scope. Where the widget renders one of those — the Help tab, a pre-chat form, the CSAT star row — the row is here; the thing being rendered is not mine to design.
  • Acceptance criteria AC-01 to AC-10 and the technical rows T-01 to T-09 are build obligations, not screens. They are not checklist rows, and I will not claim them as designed.
  • Doc 13 §19's future expansion — customer portal, co-browsing, web push, native SDK — is explicitly deferred and absent here.
