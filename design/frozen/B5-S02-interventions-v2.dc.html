Interventions and explainability

Everything a supervisor does from the live board, and the record that explains what the router did on its own. Doc 15 FR-11 is explicit that these execute with their owning module's semantics — so none of these dialogs invents a confirmation pattern, and each one is attributed to the human who pressed it.

One idea runs through all of it: a machine decision is never final and never mysterious. Every override says whose it was, and every automatic routing choice can be read back as numbered steps in plain language. Continues from B5-S01; chrome and tokens are B1-DS verbatim.

C14 · Confirmation weight matches consequence
Undoable Force status, single reassign — execute immediately with a toast that offers undo. Interrupting a supervisor mid-crisis for a reversible act is its own failure.
Visible to others Broadcast, pull-in — one dialog stating who is affected, because other people will see the result.
Structural Anything that moves many tickets at once — the restructure wizard's typed confirmation, specified in Doc 11 FR-18 and delivered with the Organization screens.

Pressing Undo runs the opposite action through the same machinery that ran the first one: a second reassignment back, attributed to you, timestamped, in the audit trail. Nothing is rewritten and nothing disappears — the ticket carries both moves. Where the inverse is not legal the control is present but disabled with the reason, so the absence of undo is explained rather than silent.

§F

375 — fully executable, every action confirmed

row C02 · owner ruling, v2

Your revised ruling: every intervention runs on a phone, with a confirmation step to catch mis-taps. That makes the confirmation the safety mechanism the fat-finger policy used to provide by absence — so it is designed as a real component, not a browser-style “are you sure”.

Four rules make it work: the confirm restates the consequence in nouns — who, how many, until when — rather than repeating the button label; the primary button carries the specific verb, never “OK”; destructive and reversible actions are visually different weights; and the confirm button sits on the right at 44px minimum, away from the thumb's resting arc on the cancel side.
