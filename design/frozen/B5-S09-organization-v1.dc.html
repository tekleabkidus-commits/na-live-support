B5-S09-organization-v1 checklist J01–J15

Backoffice → Organization

Doc 11 §8. The same supervisor who acts in seconds on the live floor comes here to restructure carefully — so this surface is deliberately slower: inventory before action, warnings that name the specific misconfiguration, and nothing destructive without a list of what breaks.

The strategy panels are the heart of it. A routing strategy is invisible machinery until something goes wrong, so each panel is built as a preview of what will actually happen rather than a form of settings — you see where the rotation pointer sits, who gets skipped, and what a customer will be told.

Rows J16–J21, the restructure and delete wizards, follow in B5-S10 — they are a flow of their own and carry your v3 mobile ruling.

Each panel answers "what will happen" rather than "what is configured". The starvation row is the design decision I most want your eye on: Doc 11 FR-12 makes it non-negotiable, so it sits pinned and locked inside the drag list rather than in a footnote — a supervisor reordering boosts needs to see the rule that outranks all of them.

Delivery note

Delivered: J01–J15, both themes. Three tabs, departments list and detail with the danger zone, teams spanning departments with role-context chips, queues grouped by department with strategy badges and temporary-member countdowns, the specific warning badge, all three strategy panels, the priority-boost list with the pinned starvation rule, the VIP source warning, membership and routing tags, the RG special case, the no-eligible-agents breakdown, the estimator preview, and loading / conflict-on-save.

One judgement call: the failed save is drawn as an edit conflict rather than a generic error. Doc 11 §8 asks for "a failed save" without saying which failure — a concurrent edit is the one that actually happens in a shared admin, and it needs a different resolution from a network drop. Say the word if you want the generic version too.
