C07 · The onboarding that isn't here

Doc 13 §8 First-open

No tour, no coach marks, no “welcome to our new chat”, no dismissible tips. Doc 13 calls this out as a deliberate anti-pattern rejection, and it is worth stating in the design rather than leaving as an omission someone helpfully fills in later. A stranger with a missing deposit will not read a tutorial. The greeting, the wait band and three buttons are the whole of it.
