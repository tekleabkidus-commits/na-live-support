The enrolment wizard

Three steps, on the profile security page, and the third one carries the only thing in this product that is shown exactly once. Everything in the design bends toward that: the recovery codes step is the widest, the plainest, the hardest to skip past, and the only step whose continue button starts disabled.

The tone matters as much as the layout. “Shown exactly once” has to read as a fact being handed over, not as a threat — someone who feels warned rushes, and rushing is how the codes end up unsaved. So the step states what will happen, gives two ways to keep them, and only then lets the person move on.

The page chrome around the wizard is Doc 07's workspace shell and is Batch 1 law — shown here for context only, never rebuilt. Amharic is real text, flagged for native review.

§C

Step 2, regeneration, and the two-tab collision

C03 · C07 · C09 · Doc 02 FR-06, FR-07, SEC-03, EC-10

Step 2 exists to prove the authenticator actually works before anything is switched on, so its failure state stays on the step and never rolls the person back to the QR. Regeneration reuses step 3 exactly — same grid, same once-only sentence, same blocked continue — with one extra line stating that the previous eight stop working the moment the new set appears.

Why this state says more than “try again”

Doc 02 EC-10 requires a clear invalidation error. The trap it does not mention: the loser may already have scanned the discarded QR, leaving a dead “Selam Bet Support” entry in their authenticator that produces codes forever and never works. Six months later that entry is why someone thinks two-factor is broken. One sentence here prevents a support ticket nobody would ever connect back to this moment.

§D

375 — enrolling on the phone that holds the app

C10 · Doc 02 NFR-05, §9

On a phone the QR is nearly useless — the camera and the code are on the same screen — so the order inverts: the setup key comes first with a copy button that hands it straight to the authenticator, and the QR drops below for the person scanning from a second device. Step 3 keeps all eight codes on one screen without a scroll trap: the grid goes single-column, download comes before copy because a file survives a lost phone, and the tick box and finish button stay above the fold.
