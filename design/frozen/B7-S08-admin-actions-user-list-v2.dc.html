v2 — what expansion broke, and what I changed

The disable consequence sentence became a list. In English it is one sentence: sessions and tokens revoked at the same moment. The Amharic runs long enough that as a paragraph it read as a wall at 1.85 line-height, and the atomic-revocation fact — the one an administrator must not miss — sat mid-sentence. It is now three bullets, and the English v1 paragraph stays as it is: this is a language-specific structure, not a rewrite of the approved English.

The action grid and the dialog footer both wrap now. Four action cards at fixed height clipped the two-factor card's Amharic; the grid is content-height. The dialog's button row wraps with the destructive action pushed to the far end by its own margin, so it is never adjacent to “ተወው”.

The typed confirmation takes the Amharic name. “ዮናስ ተስፋዬ” is what the field expects — asking an Amharic-reading administrator to type a Latin transliteration to confirm a destructive act would be a trap, not a check.

No Amharic string was shortened to fit a frame. H stays ❌ in the audit until your deferred clearance line covers it.
