Baseline: the built resources/js/Pages/Workflow/Hours.vue (click-to-toggle 30-minute grid cells as the enhancement, the interval-list editor as the accessible requirement, both editing the same hours; holidays with recurring toggles; the department-override diff line; the prospective notice; timezone labelled), refined. Differs from build: (1) the grid is a real painter — a drag selection with a live announcement — where the build toggles one cell per click; (2) the override diff is a side-by-side day table with changed rows marked, beside the built one-line diff (“Payments differs: Sat closed”), which is kept as the summary sentence; (3) calendars are cards with their scope and a one-line summary, where the build lists them as rows; (4) holidays carry real Ethiopian fixtures with the recurring flag honestly split (fixed dates every year; Fasika and the Eids move); (5) the 24:00 close and the never-crosses-midnight rule are drawn on the Night desk calendar.

Designed absences: nothing retroactive — the banner on the edit screen names both facts (changes apply from now; armed clocks keep their calendar) and no control reaches an armed instance (BR-03, EC-05, X4); no interval crossing midnight (two intervals instead); no colour-only meaning — painted cells carry the day summary in words and the list is the same data; no animation; no starvation cap anywhere (X1).

H01 H02 H03 H05Three calendar cards (default · Payments override · a draft Night desk) and the editor for the default. The BR-03 / EC-05 banner names both facts first. Name, the tenant timezone labelled explicitly (also printed on the painter), scope. THE PAINTER: 7 rows × 48 half-hour cells, open cells tinted with a bar, the day summary in words beside each row; Wednesday is a split shift (08–12 · 14–20), Saturday a short day, Sunday closed. THE INTERVAL LIST under it edits the same hours: open → close fields per interval, Remove, Add interval, “Closed all day” — fully keyboard operable, HH:MM, 24:00 = end of day. Holidays: date, name, the recurring switch with its word (Every year / This date only), Remove, and the add row. Save states the effect on the 4 policies that reference this calendar.
