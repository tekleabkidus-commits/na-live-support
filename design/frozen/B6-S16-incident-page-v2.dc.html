The incident page

Four rows, and the whole batch's mobile rule is really about this one screen. It is designed for a specific person: an operator woken at two in the morning, holding a phone in one hand, who needs to stop something in under a minute and be able to explain it at nine. Every decision here follows from that.

So: one page, not three linked by a breadcrumb — AI kill switches, rules-pause and module flags together, because in an incident you do not know yet which one you need. The consequence is named before the tap, never after. And the page keeps a plain-language record of what is currently stopped, who stopped it and when, so the morning after reads itself.

Components are B1-DS verbatim; the flags themselves are B6-S13's, surfaced here rather than rebuilt.
