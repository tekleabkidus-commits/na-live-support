Roles — list, compare, lifecycle

Section G is the densest part of the batch, so I am delivering it in two halves. This is the first: the list an admin lands on, comparing two roles, and the whole lifecycle around a role — cloning it, deleting it, the seeded templates, and the two warnings the documents attach. The permission matrix itself, the scopes, the impact modal and their states are the second half.

The one number that shapes this list is users affected. Every action here — clone, edit, delete — is about people who are working right now, so the count is on the row, on the delete dialog, and on the compare view. Nothing about roles is ever presented as if it only touched configuration.

Tables, buttons, pills, skeletons and diff colours are B1-DS verbatim. Staff names and role names are NA BET PLC's; permission keys are Doc 03 §4.1's, unaltered.
