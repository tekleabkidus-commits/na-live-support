Everything on this page narrows what the assistant may do — and the page is honest about which narrowings belong to whom. Platform-seeded rules wear a lock: the operator may reword how the assistant declines, never whether it declines. The RG template gets the gravest treatment on the page, because it is the one text the machine must deliver exactly as a human wrote it, byte for byte, in the customer's language, every time.

Seeded lists grow (an operator can always block more) and never shrink below the platform floor. The Backoffice shell is B6-S11 law — context only. Amharic is real, flagged for native review.
