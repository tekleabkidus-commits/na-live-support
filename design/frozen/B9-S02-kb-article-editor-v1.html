⋮⋮Heading
When finance reverses a credited deposit
⋮⋮Paragraph
A deposit is reversed when the payment provider settles a different amount than the one credited, or when the transaction is flagged by the AML desk. Do not tell the customer why until finance confirms the reason code.
⋮⋮Callout
Never quote a reversal reason from this page to a customer. Hand off to finance with the ticket link.
⋮⋮Numbered list
  1. Open the ticket, add the internal note “reversal — awaiting finance”.
  2. Check the settlement report for the transaction ID.
  3. Escalate to Payments with priority High.
