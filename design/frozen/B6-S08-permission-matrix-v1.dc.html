The permission matrix

Section G's second half, and the hardest screen in the batch: 113 checkboxes that have to stay comprehensible. Three things carry it. Scope is settled first, at the top, in a sentence — “can see tickets belonging to: their department” — because a permission means nothing until you know over which records. The matrix is then grouped by module with a tri-state header, so an admin works a module at a time rather than a list of a hundred. And the summary stays visible, counting people rather than permissions, because that is the number that matters when you press Save.

Every key shown is verbatim from Doc 03 §4.1. Tables, checkboxes, shields, diff colours, skeletons and toasts are B1-DS; the shell and roles list are B6-S07's, unchanged.
