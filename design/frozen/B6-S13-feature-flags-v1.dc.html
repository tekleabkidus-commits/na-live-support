Feature flags

This is the screen someone opens at two in the morning with a channel misbehaving, and the design has one job: make the cost of the switch visible before it is flipped, and make the promise that nothing is destroyed believable. Every card carries live usage rather than a description, because “Telegram: 3 bots, ~1,200 conversations a day” is the number that decides whether you flip it, and no amount of prose substitutes.

Off is stated honestly and in the same three clauses everywhere: the navigation goes, the API answers feature_disabled, and new work parks rather than vanishing. The parked count is live on the card while the module is off, so an operator can see the queue building and knows exactly what drains when they turn it back on.

Components are B1-DS verbatim; the shell and the per-key anatomy are B6-S11's.

§B

Confirms and failure states — each in both themes

rows L04, L06, L08, L10, L12 · Doc 16 FR-12, BR-04, EC-10, FS-03

Disabling names its dependents; enabling a dependent offers to bring its dependency with it rather than failing. The last-channel case is allowed and says what it costs. And when the flag store itself is unreachable, the switches freeze where they are and say why — a flag screen that guesses is worse than one that admits it cannot reach the truth.

Deviations to record: the 375 board is operable, not read-only. Doc 16 §9's own mobile exception for the confirm dialog implies the toggle it confirms is reachable there, and a stop button you cannot press from a phone is not a stop button. Switch targets at 375 are 52 × 30 px, above the floor.

One thing I want your eye on: every card states usage as a live number rather than a description of the feature. It reads as an operations screen instead of a settings page, which I think is right for something people touch during incidents — but it does mean each card depends on the realtime tier, and a card with no number would look broken. Worth deciding what a card shows when its usage query times out; my instinct is the last known figure with its age, never a blank.
