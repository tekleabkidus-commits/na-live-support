B6-S02-telegram-connections-v1 rows C01–C15

Business connection lifecycle

This is the screen someone works standing next to the support phone — Doc 05 §9 says so outright, and it is the reason the checklist is designed phone-first and the desktop version is the same thing with more room. Everything reused is B1-DS verbatim: the connection-state pills, channel-health dots, tables, typed-confirmation pattern, skeletons and toasts. The shell is B6-S01's, unchanged.

The design problem here is that three of the four things that go wrong happen on the phone, not in this UI — a wrong account, a missing reply permission, someone tapping disconnect in Telegram. So each state names what happened on the phone and what to do there, rather than reporting an abstract status. The other rule the screen obeys: an unconfirmed connection never routes a single message, and it says so where an admin can see it.

Amharic is real text, flagged for native review. Accounts and staff are NA BET PLC's.

Not here, by design: the rescue view itself is Doc 07 and Batch 1 law — this screen links to it and states what lands there. Health metrics, the raw-update viewer and test tools are Section F. The reply-window setting is Section D of the contract.

One new token, proposed — not baked in. Doc 05 SEC-04 and EC-02 require a connection that exists but has not been confirmed, and B1-DS §2 fixes exactly three connection-state pills: Active, Paused, Revoked. A pending line is none of those — it is not healthy, it is not an owner-side pause, and calling it Paused would be a lie about who is holding it. So C03 introduces a fourth pill, ◔ Pending in light and ◔ Pending in dark, built from B1-DS's existing neutral strip colours rather than a new hue — deliberately the quietest pill in the set, because a pending line is doing nothing. If you approve it, it is promoted to a B1-DS addendum and reused from there, never re-drawn by eye. Reject it and I will bring you alternatives.

Deviations to record: the pending pill above, pending your ruling. Doc 05 §9 asks for the connection tab at tablet and the checklist on the phone; both are delivered, the 375 checklist is fully operable, and every control on it is at or above 44 px.

One thing I want your eye on: the disconnect dialog lists the stranded tickets before the confirmation field rather than after it. It makes the dialog tall — on a phone it is a scroll — and that is the intent. Tell me if you would rather have a count with a "show tickets" disclosure, and I will change it; it is the only place in the screen where I deliberately spent height on friction.
