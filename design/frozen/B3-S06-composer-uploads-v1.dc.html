G10 · The upload a keyboard can complete

Drag and paste are conveniences layered on top of a plain file input, never replacements for it. Tab reaches ＋, Enter opens the menu, arrow keys move through Photo and Document, Enter opens the system picker, and the attachment card that comes back has its own focusable remove button. Nothing in this flow requires a pointer, and the drop zone is decoration for people who happen to have one.
