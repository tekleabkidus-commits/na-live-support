AI marking & honesty

B8-S03-ai-marking-honesty-v1 · Section C · checklist rows C01–C13 (+ X2 X3 X4 audited) · Doc 09 FR-19a–d, FR-9/10/12/13, BR-02/03/05, EC-01/02/04/08, AC-05 · §8 §9 §14

This screen is the platform's conscience drawn out: what the customer reads when a machine answers them about their money, and what staff see so no AI word ever passes as a human's. The rules it renders are absolute — the assistant introduces itself as an AI and that introduction has no off switch; money facts are only ever the customer's own claim, restated; grounded answers cite or the turn hands off; RG moments get one fixed, human-approved text, never generation; and an AI message is never edited after send — a correction is a new message, visibly.

Customer frames show OUR CONTENT inside Telegram's own chrome (drawn neutrally — Telegram's UI is not ours to design). Staff frames use Doc 07's thread with the B1-DS AI bubble verbatim. The persona "Selam" and @BrandSupport are white-label-neutral fixtures. Amharic is real, flagged for native review.

§I — Honesty contract (build notes on the frame)
X2 X3 X4 audited · AC-05 · §13 §14
Contract
· X3: the disclosure appears in every first greeting, every language; no frame on any Batch 8 screen offers a control that hides it — wording is operator content, presence is platform law.
· X4: every money sentence in every frame is the customer's claim restated ("you mentioned", "your receipt shows") or a cited process fact; the trap exchange is drawn refusing.
· X2: the RG frame's text is the operator-approved template, byte-fixed; the staff flag always carries its excerpt; slate treatment, never alarm red.
· Corrections are new messages; AI bubbles carry no edit affordance in any state (FR-19d).
· The AI accent is never the only marker — the "✦ AI assistant" text label rides every AI artifact (§9, never color alone).
· Customer frames are 340 px content in Telegram's chrome — mobile-first by nature; staff thread meets Doc 07's own responsive law. All Amharic flagged for native review (R-14).
