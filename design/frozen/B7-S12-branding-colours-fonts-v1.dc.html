Branding — Colours & Fonts

The group where an operator's brand meets the accessibility floor, and the only validator in the platform with no way past it. Doc 16 BR-02 is absolute: a failing pair blocks publish, the editor offers the nearest passing shade, and there is no “publish anyway” in any state — not in the matrix, not in the publish dialog, not in an error or an offline state. That absence is designed, and §C shows every place it was checked for.

This is one validator with two consumers. Doc 13's widget designer shipped its refusal in Batch 3, frozen at B3-S08-widget-designer-v3; the card below is that component read out of the source file — same hues, same sentence shape, same apply-the-suggestion button — not a lookalike rebuilt from memory.

Built on B1-DS v4. Shell is Section J's. Editing is desktop per Doc 16 §9; 375 is the read-only rendering.

L01 · L02 · L03 · L04 · Doc 16 FR-01, FR-04, §9, §13

§C

No override — every state I checked, and the colours a tenant does not own

L05 · L06 · L02 · L07
Where a “publish anyway” could have hidden
Six states, each checked and each without one: the matrix row (no per-row dismiss); the failing-pair card (one button, and it applies the fix); the group header (publish is disabled at source, not intercepted later); the publish dialog (Section S); the save-failed error state (retry, never bypass); and offline, where the editor holds the draft rather than offering an unvalidated publish.
The one phrase that does appear is the plain statement that no override exists — because an operator who cannot find the escape hatch should be told it was never built, not left hunting.
L06 · the colours a tenant does not own
Eight token families carry meaning rather than brand, and the group says so where an operator would look for them: ticket status, priority, SLA, reply window, connection state, channel health, internal note, and the violet AI accent.
●Resolved ▲ SLA breached⏱ Window 3h 12m 🔒 Internal note ✦ AI
Every chip above is read out of B1-DS v2 §2 — Resolved #DEF4F1/#0A7266, SLA breached #FBE5E5/#B32B2B, reply window #EDEEF2/#4A5460 — not matched by eye from a neighbouring surface. A tenant whose brand colour is teal still gets the same teal for “resolved”, and the AI accent stays violet everywhere: Doc 09 BR-05’s disclosure rule is not a style. White-label extends to identity, never to meaning.
L02 · four pairings, and the absent control
Inter + Noto Sans Ethiopic · Source Sans 3 + Noto Sans Ethiopic · IBM Plex Sans + Noto Sans Ethiopic · system stack + Noto Sans Ethiopic. Every pairing carries the Ethiopic face, because a tenant cannot choose a font that cannot render half its customers' language.
Custom upload is Doc 16 §19 — deferred, and stated in the group rather than left as a missing control someone hunts for.

Delivery note

Delivered: all seven rows. L01–L04 in §A; L05 and L07 in §B at 1440 dark in Amharic, where the refusal sentence runs longest; L02, L05, L06 and L07 in §C; the read-only 375 in §D.

Two places carried the same recollection, and both are corrected. The §A preview’s Resolved badge rendered in #E4F4EA/#156F45 with a ✓ — the widget list’s Live pill — where the ticket-status token is #DEF4F1/#0A7266 with a ● at 3 × 9. It now matches §C exactly. The validator’s own “14 of 14 pairs pass” chip also wore the Open status fill for a meaning that is not a ticket state; it is now a neutral pill. A sweep of all thirteen Batch 7 files found no other instance.

The fixed-token panel is quoted, not recalled. A first pass showed “Resolved” in #E4F4EA/#156F45 and “SLA breached” in #8E2222 — the first is the widget list’s Live pill and the second the generic error hue, both borrowed from adjacent surfaces. The real values are Resolved #DEF4F1/#0A7266 and SLA breached #FBE5E5/#B32B2B, now read from B1-DS v2 §2 along with the reply-window chip. Asserting a fixed token by eye, on the one panel claiming those tokens cannot be re-mixed, is the drift B12-R01 exists to prevent.

Every ratio in this file is computed, not estimated. A first pass carried approximated numbers and a suggestion shade that over-corrected — #7A5C05 would have landed at 6.3:1, which is safe but is not nearest, and nearest is the property Doc 16 FR-04 actually specifies. The suggestion is now #91722A at 4.52:1, the first shade of that hue to clear the threshold, and the six matrix ratios are recomputed. On a surface whose whole subject is the validator’s credibility, an approximate number is a bug.

Shared-validator consistency, checked against source. The refusal card takes B3-S08's surface, border, role="alert", sentence shape and apply-button pattern verbatim. One difference, flagged: B3's apply button renders at min-height: 34px; B1-DS — now v4 as system of record — sets 36. I drew 36 and did not touch the frozen Batch 3 file. That is either a two-pixel correction to B3-S08 as a scope-limited reopen, or a deliberate widget-side metric; it is your call, and it is the only place the two consumers differ.

No override, and the six states it could have hidden in are listed in §C rather than asserted — matrix row, refusal card, group header, publish dialog, save-failed, offline. The publish button is disabled at the group header, not intercepted after a click, so the refusal is never a surprise at the end of a dialog.
