The branding editor — shell

The Doc 16 half of the batch starts here. Three panes: what you are editing on the left, the fields in the middle, and what it will look like on the right — the preview changes with the group, so a colour change and an email header are never judged from the same picture.

One rule governs the whole surface and it is counter-intuitive enough to be stated on screen: the editor is never themed by the draft. It stays in the platform's own colours while only the preview renders the tenant's. That is what makes a broken draft recoverable — you can always read the tool that fixes it.

Editing is desktop, per Doc 16 §9 and your 3 August ruling; 375 ships as the read-only rendering behind the documented notice. Tenant is Selam Bet throughout.

Delivery note

Delivered: all seven rows. J01–J05 in §A at 1440 light; J03, J06 and J07 in §B, with dark at 1440 and the read-only 375 beside them.

The 375 is a designed screen, not a blank. Per your standing ruling it renders real values, the live version, the draft state and the notice in Doc 03 §9's own words — “editing requires a larger screen” — as a first-class element rather than an apology. Deviation from Step 2's both-viewports rule is recorded here, as required.

The logo stays a slot, even inside the preview. An earlier draft of §A drew a wordmark in the preview pane; it is now the same dashed placeholder used everywhere in the batch, because B7-S01's approved treatment says the mark comes from the tenant and no screen invents one. There is a real argument that a preview showing a slot previews less than it should — if you want the preview pane alone to render an actual uploaded asset, that is a reopen of an approved treatment and yours to rule on.

One thing I want your eye on: the takeover in J06 is a button, not a request sent to Hanna. Doc 16 EC-07 says soft lock with takeover, audited — it does not describe an approval step, and inventing one would slow down the 00:15 rollback the whole system is designed for. The audit line is what keeps it honest.
