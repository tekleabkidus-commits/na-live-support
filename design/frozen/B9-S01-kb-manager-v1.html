Checklist rows: A01 · A02 · A03 · A04 · A05 · A06 · A07 · A08 · A09  |  Doc 10: §8 Manager layout · Empty states · Loading · FR-01 · FR-03 · FR-04 · FR-07 · FR-10 · FR-17 · §9 · §13 · §14 · BR-05 · BR-07

Baseline: the built resources/js/Pages/Admin/Kb/Manager.vue three-pane (categories · articles · article), refined. Differs from build: (1) the manager is drawn INSIDE the Backoffice chrome (52 px topbar + 200 px nav, B5-S09 J01) with "Knowledge base" as its own nav row — the build renders as a bare page; (2) the article rows carry the §8 columns (status pill · language dots · usage) where the build carries one badge line per variant; (3) filters and search are drawn (the build has none — §8/FR-17 require them). Tokens: B1-DS v2 both colourways, verbatim; radii 4/6/8/pill; 4 pt grid; 64 px comfortable rows; Inter + Noto Sans Ethiopic.

Designed absences: no "verified" anywhere; internal articles are ABSENT for a viewer without kb.view_internal (never masked); no delete affordance on an archived article (archive keeps its tombstone). Nothing by color alone — every dot pairs a glyph with a text tooltip; every pill carries its word.
