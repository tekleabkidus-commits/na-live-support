B1-DS v3 addendum — Batch 6 components

Seven components Batch 6 needed and B1-DS v2 did not contain. Promoted here so Batch 7 reuses them from the system rather than matching them by eye — the failure that produced the 79-correction drift report. Additive only: nothing approved is restyled, no fixed token is re-mixed, and every metric below is measured from the shipped Batch 6 files rather than redrawn.

Change note at the foot. Each entry gives metrics, states and the composition contract — what it may contain, what it must not, and where it is already in use.

Change note — v2 → v3

Added: seven components, listed above, each with metrics, states in both themes, and a composition contract. Changed: nothing. Removed: nothing. No approved element is restyled and no fixed token — status pill hues, priority dots, SLA chip, reply-window chip, connection state, channel health, internal-note tokens, or the violet AI accent — is re-mixed or redefined here.

Provenance: every metric is measured from the shipped Batch 6 files rather than redrawn — S08 (01), S09 (02), S12 (03), S15 (04), S13 (05, 07), S16 (06). Keyboard states for 01 and 04 are drawn in S08-v2 §K and S15-v2 §K, each with its acceptance-list row.

Authority: promoted on the owner's ruling of 3 August 2026 — “promote all seven to additive-only B1-DS-v3 with metrics, states, composition contracts, and a change note; 56 px button records its deliberate floor exceedance; Batch 7 waits for v3.” This document is that promotion, and Batch 7's entry condition 2 is its freeze.

Standing rule this closes: Batch 7 takes these seven from here, verbatim, and never by eye from a Batch 6 screen. The 56 px stop button carries its floor exceedance in the contract itself, so a future reuse cannot inherit the metric without inheriting the reason.
