Channel health & observability

The page someone opens when a player says “the bot is dead”. Every number on it is a diagnostic that changes what you do next — and the screen is ordered by that: what is wrong, since when, what it is costing, what to press. The action sits with the symptom rather than in a toolbar, because at 2 AM nobody reads a toolbar.

Health dots, banners, tables, skeletons and buttons are B1-DS verbatim; the shell is B6-S01's; the throughput bars use the ink ramp approved with the B5 chart set, static and never colour-only.

One placement question sits at the end of this note: the documents never say what screen size the health panel needs.

One question, and it is the only thing outstanding on this screen: should the health panel be phone-operable for that one repair, as drawn — or read-only at 375 like settings, with the repair reserved for a tablet? The case for as-drawn is that a silent webhook is the most common Telegram outage and the fix is idempotent. The case against is consistency with your ruling that the documents hold.

And one thing I want your eye on: the throughput bars use the approved ink ramp rather than a red “bad” colour, with the collapse shown by the bars going flat and stated in words underneath. Colour is not carrying the message. If you would rather the outage hours were tinted like the SLA-breach token, that is a one-line change — but it would be the first time a chart in this system used a state colour as a category.
