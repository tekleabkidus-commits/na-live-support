Dashboard builder

Doc 15 FR-13's constraint is the design idea: invalid combinations are unbuildable, not error-prone. The registry decides what the inspector offers, so a widget that mixes a live number with a historical one, or shows an AI resolution rate alone, simply cannot be produced — no validation message, no error state, the option is not there.

The metric picker is the moment the semantic dictionary stops being a document and becomes something a person uses. It is the most important control on this screen, so it gets a real search with descriptions rather than a dropdown of keys.

Doc 15 §9 sets a 1024 px floor for building under the fat-finger policy. You have overridden it. The 375 builder is fully operable, with grid position controls in place of free drag and a confirmation on every destructive edit.

Delivery note

▲ Deliberate deviation from Doc 15 §9, on your v3 ruling

Doc 15 §9 sets a 1024 px floor for building under the fat-finger policy. The 375 builder here is fully operable. What replaces the policy: the canvas metaphor is dropped for an ordered list, every control is 44px or larger, and destructive edits confirm with the share impact named.

Delivered: H01–H16, both themes. Palette, 12-column canvas with drag and resize, constrained inspector, automatic tier flag, target bands, comparison control, sharing, as-viewer preview, restricted placeholder, the honest-triad refusal, needs-update and deleted-filter widgets, unsaved guard, keyboard grid control, the 375 builder, and loading / empty / registry-error.

Judgement call: at 375 I dropped the canvas rather than shrinking it. A 12-column grid at 375 gives 28px columns — free drag there is not a fat-finger risk, it is unusable. The list is the same data model with a different control surface, so a board built on a phone opens identically on a desktop. Next: Section I — exports and schedules.
