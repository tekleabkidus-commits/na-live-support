B5-S02-interventions-v1 checklist C01–C14

Interventions and explainability

Everything a supervisor does from the live board, and the record that explains what the router did on its own. Doc 15 FR-11 is explicit that these execute with their owning module's semantics — so none of these dialogs invents a confirmation pattern, and each one is attributed to the human who pressed it.

One idea runs through all of it: a machine decision is never final and never mysterious. Every override says whose it was, and every automatic routing choice can be read back as numbered steps in plain language. Continues from B5-S01; chrome and tokens are B1-DS verbatim.

§D

The routing-decision record

C09 · C10 · C11 · Doc 11 FR-17

Doc 11 FR-17 asks for numbered steps, monospace inputs and plain-language outcomes. The reason it matters is escalation: when a customer complains that they waited while someone else was served, this is the artefact that answers them. So it copies as text.

Light

Why this ticket went to Dawit 19:12:04
1 The ticket arrived in Deposits, matched by the customer's message intent.intent=payment.deposit · confidence=0.91 · channel=telegram
2 Priority boosts were applied in order. None matched, so it took its place by arrival time.urgent=no · vip=no · window_closing=no · sla_at_risk=no · reopened=no
3 Deposits uses round robin. The rotation pointer was on ሃና, who was at capacity, so it moved on.strategy=round_robin · pointer=hana.g · load=5/5 → skipped
4 Dawit was next in rotation, online and had room, so the ticket went to him.assignee=dawit.b · load=3/5 → 4/5 · elapsed=142ms

1 Verification routes by skill. This customer writes in Amharic, so an Amharic-speaking agent is required.strategy=skill_based · require=lang:am · source=conversation_language
2 No agent with that skill is online right now.eligible=2 · online=0

A supervisor can override: pull in an Amharic speaker, or assign anyway and accept the language mismatch.
C10 · The starvation jump, and a reopened ticket's courtesy hold
◷ This ticket waited 30 minutes, so it jumped ahead of newer higher-priority tickets. This rule is always on and cannot be turned off.boost=starvation · waited=30m01s · position 7 → 1
↩ Reopened. It was held 5 minutes for Sara, who handled it before, then released to the queue when she did not pick it up.courtesy_hold=sara.b · window=5m · expired 19:33 → requeued

Both are cases where the queue order looks wrong from outside. The record is what makes them defensible instead of arbitrary.

§E

Rescue alerts and confirmation weight

C12 · C13 · C14 · Doc 11 FR-16, FR-16a, FR-19

The Rescue view itself is Batch 1's, approved and frozen. What belongs to this batch is the alert that raises it — and crucially, whether auto-return is on, because that single setting decides whether the tickets are already safe or still stranded.

Light
C12 · C13 · Three rescue conditions, and what each says about auto-return
19:33 ⚑ Heartbeat lostአቤል ጸጋዬ has been unreachable 11 min with 4 live tickets. Auto-return is off — they are still assigned to him until you act.Open Rescue →
19:21 ⚑ Channel unreachableThe Telegram connection for Bonuses stopped responding. 3 conversations cannot receive replies. Auto-return is on — they went back to the queue at 19:23.Connection health →
18:47 ⚑ Orphaned by deletionThe Promotions queue was deleted with 2 tickets still open. They have no queue and will not be routed until you place them.Place these tickets →

Each alert states the setting in force. Without it a supervisor cannot tell a handled situation from an unhandled one, which is exactly the confusion Doc 11 FR-16a exists to prevent.

C14 · Confirmation weight matches consequence
Undoable Force status, single reassign — execute immediately with a toast that offers undo. Interrupting a supervisor mid-crisis for a reversible act is its own failure.
Visible to others Broadcast, pull-in — one dialog stating who is affected, because other people will see the result.
Structural Anything that moves many tickets at once — the restructure wizard's typed confirmation, specified in Doc 11 FR-18 and delivered with the Organization screens.
#4812 reassigned to Dawit Bekele
OPTION, not in spec: the undo toast. Doc 15 §8 says confirmations follow each module's own semantics and is silent on undo. I think it is right for the reversible tier on a live floor, but it is an addition — cut it and those actions simply execute.
