§B

Refusals at the upload, not at the publish

K03 · K05 · Doc 16 FR-01, FR-04, SEC-06

Three constraints, three refusals, and each one names the number it failed on and by how much. “Invalid file” is what makes an operator email support; “1.8 MB, and the limit is 512 KB” is what makes them export again at the right size. The sanitisation notice is different in kind — nothing failed, but the file that was stored is not the file that was sent, and saying so is the honest posture for an upload that silently rewrites its input.

Logo — light backgrounds
⚠selam-bet-hero.png is 1.8 MB. The limit is 512 KB. Export it again at 512 × 128, or send the SVG — a vector is usually a tenth the size and sharper on every screen.
⚠brand-guide.pdf isn't an image. SVG or PNG only.

§C

A tenant on its first afternoon

K06 · Doc 16 §8 · FS-01

A fresh tenant is not empty — provisioning seeds a working theme, so the platform is branded-but-generic from minute one and nothing is ever broken-looking. The empty state is therefore a checklist rather than a void: four things to make it yours, in the order they change what a customer sees, with the seeded values shown as real current values rather than greyed placeholders.
