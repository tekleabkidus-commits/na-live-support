The same sections as the panel in a room twice the size — plus the two things that only exist here: the Audit tab, and the tombstone a merged profile becomes. Components come from B1-DS and B4-S01; nothing is re-mixed.

The page is not a bigger panel. In the panel an agent is mid-conversation and wants the answer to one question. Here they arrived deliberately, usually to decide something — whether to merge, whether to block, whether this is even the right person — so history, files and the audit trail get room the slot could never give them.
