Data honesty, export, erasure

Checklist rows H01–H07 · Doc 08 BR-01, FR-01, FR-16, EC-09, EC-10, SEC-04, FS-03, NFR-05, §3, §9, §14 · AC-09. Small in screens, heavy in consequence. Every row here is the product declining to claim more than it knows.

The through-line: this platform verifies nobody. Anyone may contact support on behalf of another player, and the profile records who made contact and what they said — not who they are. The control point for money is the operator's own betting backoffice, and the interface has to keep saying so quietly, on every field, forever.

Why a checkmark would be worse than nothing

A verified badge here could only ever mean “an agent typed this in”. But it would be read as “the platform checked”, and an agent releasing 2,500 birr against that reading is the exact failure the no-verification stance exists to prevent.

So the design's contribution is a small grey label that never grows into a claim, and the discipline to leave the badge unbuilt when someone reasonable asks for it.
