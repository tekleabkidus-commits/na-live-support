§B

A link set complete in one language only

O04 · Doc 16 FR-04, §14

Amharic is customer-enabled at Selam Bet, so an unlabelled link is not a warning — it blocks publish. The distinction the document draws is worth keeping visible: for a language nobody is served in, missing copy warns; for a language customers actually read, it stops the publish. A footer where three links speak Amharic and one does not is worse than no Amharic at all.

All three appear in English (§A card and the 375 frame) and in Amharic (§B's constraint strip). One judgement, scoped by owner ruling of 3 August. A missing Amharic label blocks rather than warns here — and the scope is recorded so the closing audit does not find two rulings pointing opposite ways: blocks apply to the login footer’s link labels for customer-enabled languages; this does not modify the warn-and-enable rule for content coverage established in Batches 5 and 6. The difference is the cost of the fix and the visibility of the surface: four strings on the front door, against a translation campaign behind a working fallback chain. The block names the exact link and opens its field, per the program’s pattern that a gate must carry its own fix.
