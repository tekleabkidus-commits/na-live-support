Doc 15 FR-02 names every metric, and the semantic dictionary is law: nothing here is invented, renamed or merged. What this screen decides is the editorial question the document leaves open — which metric leads each family, and which chart type earns the primary slot.

Each family is shown as its main content column at the width it occupies inside the approved shell. The chrome, filter bar, formula disclosures and drill-down are settled in B5-S05 and are not redrawn here. Agent is the worked example in that file — the seven others follow.

Delivery note

Delivered: G01–G13. Seven families here plus Agent in B5-S05, both themes. Every metric name comes from Doc 15 FR-02 verbatim; the honest triad (G06), active-time derivation (G02), cost gating (G11), the merged-ticket footnote (G12) and event-time attribution (G13) are all placed on the families that own them.

The editorial calls, which the document leaves to us: Team/Queue leads on backlog rather than volume; Customer leads on the satisfaction distribution rather than the average; AI puts the honest triad above everything else in a bordered group so it cannot be screenshotted apart; Live Chat gives the primary slot to the estimator honesty check; SMS splits everything by gateway because the aggregate hides the fault; SLA leads on pause composition as the second chart. Any of these can be reordered — they are judgement, not law.

375, drawn not inherited: §D carries all seven families at phone width. Drawing them surfaced three real divergences from the F17 pattern — SMS per-gateway cards, Telegram one row per connection, and the AI triad held as one bordered group when stacked — none of which would have been visible under an inheritance claim.
