Branding — Login

The group that reaches back into Section A: everything edited here lands on the card five hundred agents open at shift start. So the preview is not a mock of that card — it is that card, rendered under the draft, which is the only way an operator sees what they are actually changing.

It is also the one surface in the platform that needs image-aware contrast. Everywhere else a colour sits on a known colour; here it sits on whatever photograph the operator uploaded, so the validator samples the regions the card and its text actually cover — and a palette that passes on white can still fail over a bright sky.

Built on B1-DS v4. Uploaded images render as labelled fixtures per your 3 August ruling; the slot shows in the empty state.

Delivery note

Delivered: all five rows. M01, M02 and M03 in §A at 1440 light; M04 in §B at 1440 dark in Amharic, where the sampled-region table and the scrim suggestion run longest; M05 in §C as the hostile card at full 420 px; the read-only 375 beside it.

The failure is outside the card, and that is the point. The card's own background passes at 9.4:1 — it is the footer and the language switcher, which sit directly on the photograph, that fail. A validator that only checked the card would have passed this theme, so the sampled regions are the elements' own footprints, not one average of the image.

Both languages carry the no-override sentence — corrected after review. A first pass shipped it in Amharic only and the note claimed otherwise, which is the worse of the two errors. The English refusal now sits beside §B with the same sentence, and the claim above describes what actually ships.
