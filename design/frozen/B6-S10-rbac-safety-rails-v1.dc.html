RBAC safety rails & high-risk governance

Seven rails, and every one is a real state rather than a paragraph of help text. Two ideas run through all of them. First, a block must say who can unblock it — refusing an action and leaving the operator to guess the way out is how people end up in a support ticket about their own product. Second, pending is not granted, and the interface has to keep saying so, because a request that looks like a grant is worse than no request at all.

Components are B1-DS verbatim, including the shield: an icon plus the word high-risk, never a colour on its own. The two settings rows follow the per-key row contract Doc 16 FR-08 defines — label, description, typed control, provenance, reset — so a rail that lives in Settings looks like a setting rather than a special case. Section J builds that shell next; the contract is the document's, not that screen's.

Delivery note

Delivered: I01 (last-admin hard block naming who else holds each right — §B both themes, §C dark), I02 (four-eyes pending on the role, requester view in §A light and approver view with approve/reject in §A dark, plus §C light), I03 (pending never counts as granted — stated in both §A frames, drawn as the dashed “waiting” cell in the §A light matrix, restated at 375, with the 24-hour auto-expiry visible in all three), I04 (the enable-refusal with its reason — §B both themes), I05 (rbac.strict_grant with its default-off rationale — §B both themes), I06 (rbac.override.warn_threshold at 8, default 10, showing the Modified badge, provenance line and Reset — §B both themes), I07 (shield with icon and word — throughout, and as an explicit swatch row in §B dark).

Deviations to record: the 375 approve/reject is operable rather than read-only. The request expires in 24 hours and the approver is by definition not the person already at the desk; a read-only phone view would mean requests dying of inattention. Every control clears 44 px. If you would rather it match the settings ruling exactly, it is a small change.

OPTION, not in spec: the recent-decisions table on the approver screen (§A dark, lower half) is not one of rows I01–I07 and no document asks for it. I added it because an approver deciding a high-risk grant has no other way to see whether this has been asked before, and because an expired request currently leaves no visible trace — the third row shows exactly that case. It implies two things you have not approved: a queryable 30-day decision history, and a full-record destination behind it. It is marked on the screen itself and carries no live link. Say the word and it comes out; the frame is honestly short without it.

One thing I want your eye on: the waiting permission renders as a dashed, unticked box with the word “waiting” beside it — deliberately not a ticked box in a different colour, which is how most products draw this and how admins come to believe a pending grant is live. It costs a little visual elegance in the matrix and buys an unambiguous answer to “does she have it or not”.
