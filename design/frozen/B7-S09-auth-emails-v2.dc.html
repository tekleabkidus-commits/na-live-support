The three auth emails

These are the only surfaces in the batch read outside the product, in an inbox, next to spam. They have about two seconds to prove they are genuine — which is why the brand comes from the tenant's published theme rather than from a template, why the action is a button and a visible URL, and why every one of them ends by telling the reader what to do if it wasn't them.

Bodies are designed here in full rather than left as slots — your 3 August ruling. Chrome is Doc 16 FR-01's: logo, address block, legal footer, copyright, all per language, none of it written into these templates.

Rendered at 600 px, the safe measure across mail clients. Amharic is real text, flagged for native review.

§A

Invite and reset — English

I01 · I02 · I03 · I05 · Doc 02 FR-16, FR-17, §14 · Doc 16 FR-01

The invite can be warm — someone is expecting it. The reset cannot: it may be arriving unrequested, so it opens with what happened rather than with a greeting, states the one-hour single-use window, and closes by saying that ignoring it leaves everything unchanged. Neither names a role, a department or anything else about the account, and there is no “no account here” email — the address that has no account receives nothing at all.

I03 · what this body deliberately does not contain
No name, no role, no department, no “your Selam Bet account” — nothing that would confirm who holds this address to anyone who can read the inbox. One template for every recipient, and no email at all to an address without an account. The uniform-response rule from the reset screen holds all the way into the inbox, which is where it is usually broken.
§B

New device, አማርኛ, and the dark-mode client

I04 · I07 · Doc 02 T-03, §14 · Doc 16 FR-01

The new-device email is the one people scan in a hurry, so its facts sit in a block rather than a sentence — device, place, time, one per line, scannable in the notification preview. In Amharic the same block runs three lines longer, which is why the layout stacks rather than aligning in columns. On the right, the same template as a dark-mode client renders it: mail clients invert backgrounds themselves, so the design has to survive an inversion it does not control.

§C

What comes from the theme, and what is written here

I05 · Doc 16 FR-01, T-02, SEC-02
From the tenant's published theme
Logo · header band · address block · legal footer · copyright line · footer links · sender name and address · the button's colour. All per language, all edited in the branding editor, none of it written into these three templates. Change the theme and every auth email changes with it, with no deploy.
Written here, and operator-editable
Subject lines and body copy, as auth.* keys in both languages — overridable in the translation manager Batch 6 shipped, with the shipped value always visible beside the override. Links render with their target visible, and the templates accept only the safe HTML subset: no scripts, no forms, no hidden destinations.
§D

360 px — the width most of these are actually read at

v2 · I01 · I02 · I03 · owner ruling, 3 August

My original reason for shipping no narrow frame was that an email has a client width rather than a viewport. Half right, and the wrong half mattered: the dominant client width for Selam Bet's players and agents is a phone mail app at around 360 px, so that is the honest second rendering. Same templates, reflowed — no mobile-specific template, because a second template is a second thing to keep true in two languages.

The Amharic heading wraps to two lines at 332 px of content width and the body to five. Line-height 1.85 for the Ethiopic metrics, so the wrapped lines do not collide.
What the narrow width changed

The action becomes a full-width block rather than an inline pill — at 360 px an inline button leaves a thumb guessing where the edge is, and mail clients strip hover, so the target has to be unmistakable at rest. Body type goes from 12.5 px to 13 px and the button label to 14 px, because a phone at arm's length in daylight is the real reading condition for a reset link.

What did not change: the chrome, the address block, the legal line and the plain-text alternative are the same strings from the same theme. This is one template reflowing, not a mobile variant to keep in sync.
