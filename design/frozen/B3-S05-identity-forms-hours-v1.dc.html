What is asked of a visitor before they can speak, and what happens when they arrive at three in the morning. Two rules shape every frame: a form must never be the reason someone gives up on a missing 2,500 birr, and an identity the operator's site asserts is asserted — never verified, in any wording anywhere.

F13 · Light — the site logged in as someone else

A shared phone, two brothers, one account switch mid-conversation. Doc 13 is conservative here and so is the design: nothing visible happens. The conversation continues under the person who started it, staff get a system note that the site identity changed, and the next conversation begins under the new one.

What staff see, not the visitor

Automatically re-pointing a live conversation at a different customer is how support systems leak one person's payment history to another. Doing nothing, loudly, in the audit trail is the safer failure.
