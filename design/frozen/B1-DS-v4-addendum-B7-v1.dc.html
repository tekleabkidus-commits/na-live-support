B1-DS v4 addendum — Batch 7 components

Seven components Sections A–J needed and B1-DS v2 plus the B5 and v3 addenda did not have. Every canonical form below is measured from the drawn instances, not designed here — where instances disagreed, the disagreement is named in the reconciliation note at the foot and one form is chosen with a reason.

Additive only. Nothing in v2, B5 or v3 is changed, restyled or deprecated. Each entry gives metrics, states and the composition contract — what it must do and what it must never do.

Reconciliation — where the drawn instances disagreed

Every canonical form above was measured across all ten Batch 7 files. Five of the seven were already identical everywhere they appeared. Two needed a decision, and one of those is a correction to shipped screens:

06 · meter widthCorrection. Three widths shipped: 34 px on the reset card (8 bars), 30 px on the invite card (4 bars), 28 px on the mobile reset (4 bars). Nothing justified the variation — the narrower ones were fitted by eye to their cards. Canonical is 34 × 5. S05 needs a v2 to bring the invite card and the mobile card onto it; the meter is unchanged everywhere else. 05 · segment heightKept as a rule, not a conflict. 46 px appears 54 times and 52 px twelve times, and the split is exactly desktop versus mobile — a deliberate mobile enlargement, now written into the contract rather than left as a pattern someone has to notice. 01 · field height38 px (33 instances) and 44 px (11) — desktop versus mobile plus the transcribed-field variant at S02 B07, which is 44 px on desktop too because a recovery code is read off paper. Recorded as a named variant so it cannot be mistaken for drift. 02 · toggle size32 × 30 desktop, 44 × 44 mobile, consistent everywhere. No correction. 03 · checkbox16 px desktop, 20 px mobile, consistent. No correction. 04 · dark linkCorrected during Section A review: #8FB0F5 appeared in 13 places against #A9C0F5 in the checklist. All 13 are now #A9C0F5; zero instances of the other value remain. 07 · state chipSingle instance set, S08 only. Promoted as drawn.

Change note — v3 → v4

Added: seven components, listed above, each with metrics, states and a composition contract. Changed: nothing. No v2, B5 or v3 component is restyled, renamed or deprecated; the fixed tokens — status, priority, SLA, reply-window, connection state, channel health, internal note and the violet AI accent — are untouched, and 07 is explicitly barred from reusing any of them.

Provenance: every value is measured from the shipped Batch 7 instances across S01–S10, not authored here. Where instances disagreed the reconciliation note names the disagreement, the count, and the form chosen.

One correction falls out of this: the strength meter in B7-S05 renders at three widths. It needs a v2 to sit on the canonical 34 × 5. I have not touched an approved file to make the addendum look tidy — that is a reopen and it is yours to authorise.
