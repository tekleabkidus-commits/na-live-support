Deposits & withdrawals

How long do withdrawals take?

Updated 12 July

Withdrawals to telebirr and CBE Birr normally arrive within 15 minutes of approval.

Bank transfers take up to one business day. Requests made after 17:00 are processed the next morning.

First withdrawals need a verified account, which adds a few minutes the first time only.

Withdrawal limits

Updated 3 July · English

The daily withdrawal limit is 50,000 ETB for verified accounts and 10,000 ETB before verification.

Limits reset at midnight East Africa Time. Raising a limit takes one working day and needs a document check.

Deposits & withdrawals

Why was my deposit not credited?

Updated 28 June

Most deposits appear within a minute. If yours has not, the usual cause is a reference number that did not travel with the transfer.

Send us the transaction number from your telebirr or bank receipt and we can match it manually.

Keep the receipt — a screenshot of it is the fastest way for an agent to trace the payment.

Two things this tab must not become

A deflection funnel, and a search box with nothing behind it. The footer that says “start a chat” is deliberately the most prominent control on every screen of the tab — a player chasing a missing 2,500 birr is not in the mood to read three articles, and burying the human is how a widget earns a reputation for wasting time. Deflection is a side effect of a good article, never a goal the interface pursues.
