L07 · Focus, trapped and returned
HanaEsc closes
Tab cycles inside the panel only — it never leaks into the page behind. Closing returns focus to the launcher, so a keyboard user is exactly where they were, not at the top of the site.
The focus ring is the platform's, unmodified — never removed for looks, never a faint custom outline that disappears on a bright brand colour.
L08–L10, L13 · Announced, and how often
The thread is a polite log
New messages announce their author and kind — “Selam, message” or “AI assistant, message” — and never interrupt what is already being read.
Queue position, on change only
“Now second in line” is spoken when the number actually moves. The card recomputes far more often than that, and announcing every recompute would make the widget unusable with a screen reader on.
The star row is a radiogroup
Five radios labelled one to five stars, arrow keys between them, and skipping it entirely is a legitimate answer.
Text zoom to 200%
Type is rem-based, so the panel grows with the browser's setting instead of clipping. A visitor who needs large text is often the visitor who most needs the help.
