D01 · Why there is no conversation list

Doc 13 FR-10 allows a list, and then BR-04 makes it almost always a list of one: linked-work splits, merges and new tickets are staff mechanics, and the visitor sees a single continuous conversation regardless. So Messages opens straight into the thread. The list only appears if a visitor genuinely has two separate conversations, and even then it is a thin row above the thread rather than a screen of its own — a stranger with a missing deposit should not have to choose a folder.
