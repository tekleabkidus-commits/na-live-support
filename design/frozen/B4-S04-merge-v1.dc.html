Merge

Checklist rows D01–D13 · Doc 08 FR-11, FR-12, FR-13, EC-04, EC-12, §8 Merge flow, §13, NFR-02, FS-02 · Doc 06 FR-10, FR-21. Three steps — pick, choose, confirm — then a drawer, a tombstone and thirty days to change your mind. The confirm-phrase input comes from B1-S04; the merge dialog's shape follows the one approved there.

This is the operation that consolidates two people into one, and it is irreversible in practice after a month. So the design's job is not to make it fast — it is to make sure the agent has actually understood the size of it before the button is live. Every step states a number.
