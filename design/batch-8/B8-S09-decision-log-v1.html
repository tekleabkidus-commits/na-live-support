Decision log & quality loop

B8-S09-decision-log-v1 · Section I · checklist rows I01–I06 · Doc 09 FR-20, FR-16, SEC-06, SS-02, T-08 · standing ruling Q2: exactly TWO quality tabs; the RG review queue is NOT on this screen — it lives with the supervisor surfaces

Every AI decision the platform makes lands in this table, and every row learns its own ending: resolved and stayed resolved, resolved and came back, handed off and the human agreed with the routing, a draft the agent edited before sending. That enrichment is what turns a log into a tuning instrument — the two tabs above the table are the weekly product of it: the questions the AI keeps failing to answer (each one click from becoming the KB article that fixes it), and the calibration curve that says whether the bot's confidence can be believed.

Access is split by design: this screen shows the decision record; raw prompt/output payloads sit behind the stricter platform-debug permission and self-reduce to hashes after 30 days. Per ruling Q2, no RG material appears here in any form. Backoffice shell is B6-S11 law — context only.

§H — Contract (build notes on the frame)
I01–I06 · Q2 honored · SEC-06 · §13
Contract
· Q2 honored — the designed absence: the quality loop has exactly TWO tabs. The RG precision review queue appears NOWHERE on this screen — no tab, no count, no filter value, no mention. It lives with the supervisor surfaces (B8-S10) behind the stricter RG permission.
· Outcome enrichment renders in the outcome column of the same row — the log and its endings are never two screens.
· The 30-day payload reduction is a property of the data, shown on the row chip; the payload door (platform-debug) is separate from the table door (ai.decisions.view), and payload access is itself audited (SEC-06).
· Confidence is always text with its band word; outcome chips carry words, color second (§13).
· The calibration curve always ships with its text table and a written finding; the chart alone is never the record.
· "Draft KB article" produces a Doc 10 draft — nothing publishes from this screen.
