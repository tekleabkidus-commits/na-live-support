Baseline: the built resources/js/Pages/Workflow/RuleEditor.vue + ConditionTree.vue (three panels, the recursive all/any tree with add / group / move-into / move-out as BUTTONS and aria-level, per-type operators, the keyword-set picker, ordered action cards with per-type config and the CLOSED external arms, the inline SMS validation, the dry-run drawer with ✓/✗ truth badges and evaluated values, save-as-draft vs activate, the execution log with SEC-03 elision, the ≥1024 read-only notice), refined. Differs from build: (1) the three panels are numbered and connected top-to-bottom as the execution order, in the B5-S07 builder idiom (numbered header, grip + ↑↓ on every card) — the build renders three plain sections; (2) the 13 triggers are shown as a chip row under the picker so the vocabulary is visible; (3) the schedule trigger shows the 5-minute floor and the timezone next-run preview beside the cron (the build shows the preview only when saved); (4) action cards carry one help line each naming their door (transition service, routing, Doc 14 template, allowlist, Doc 16) — the build prints help for SMS and webhook only; (5) the dry-run verdict, the ANY-group roll-up row (“1 of 2 true”) and the “first ✗ is where to look” line are drawn; the build lists leaves flat; (6) the execution log’s expanded detail with the RETRY-ACTION affordance is drawn (§8 Empty/loading/error) — the build lists executions without detail; (7) the activate confirmation is a dialog naming the atomic swap, the diff, the dry-run status and the capability floor — the build activates on click.

Designed absences: no free-text SMS body anywhere — the SMS card offers a template reference only (BR-04, X2); no typed webhook URL — destinations are the allowlist (SEC-02); no AI task other than the three names; no per-rule guard setting — 10 · 20 · 3 are printed as platform limits (BR-05, X1-adjacent: nothing about the starvation cap appears); no “assign to agent” around routing; no greyed buttons — a blocked save refuses inline; nothing by colour alone (✓/✗ carry their words); no animation.
