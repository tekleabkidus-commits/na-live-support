What a policy does

A policy names who it matches (department, priority, channel, tags, VIP), sets three targets per priority, picks a clock (business hours from a calendar, or 24 × 7), says when the clock pauses, and what happens when priority changes mid-stage. The first policy that matches a new ticket arms its clocks with a snapshot; the chip on the ticket is that snapshot counting down.
Seeded content elsewhere keeps empties rare; this one is real until you write the first policy.
